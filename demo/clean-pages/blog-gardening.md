# Five Tips for a Better Vegetable Garden

1. **Start small.** A 2 m by 2 m bed is plenty for a first season.
2. **Feed the soil.** Add compost every spring.
3. **Water deeply, not often.** Roots grow down when water does.
4. **Mulch.** It keeps weeds down and moisture in.
5. **Plant what you eat.** Tomatoes, courgettes and salad leaves are forgiving.
