# Lemon Olive Oil Cake

A moist, simple cake. Serves 8. Takes about 55 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 3 eggs, 1 cup sugar, 3/4 cup olive oil, zest and juice of 2 lemons
- 1 1/2 cups flour, 1 tsp baking powder, pinch of salt

## Method
Whisk eggs and sugar, stream in the oil, fold in the dry ingredients and lemon. Bake at 175C for 40 minutes.
